Refund & Cancellation Policy for IntegriBiz

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how subscriptions to IntegriBiz ("we," "our," or "us") can be cancelled and when refunds are available for our mobile and web applications (collectively, the "Services"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law, including the South African Consumer Protection Act and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

Free Trial

New accounts may start with a 5-day free trial. No payment is taken during the trial. If you do not want to continue, cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged.

Cancelling Your Subscription

  • How to Cancel

    You can cancel at any time from your account settings in the IntegriBiz app, or by emailing support@integribiz.com from the email address linked to your account.

  • When Cancellation Takes Effect

    Cancellation stops future billing. You keep access to paid features until the end of the billing period you have already paid for, after which your account returns to a non-paid state.

  • Your Data After Cancellation

    We recommend exporting your documents before your paid period ends. After cancellation, your data is retained and deleted as described in our Privacy Policy.

  • App Store Purchases

    If you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, cancellations and refunds are handled by that store under its own policies. Please manage your subscription through your store account.

Refunds

  • Cooling-Off Period

    If you subscribe to a paid plan for the first time, you may cancel within 7 days of your first payment and receive a full refund of that payment.

  • Monthly Subscriptions

    Outside the cooling-off period, monthly subscription fees (such as the SME Plan) are non-refundable, and we do not issue refunds or credits for partial months or unused time.

  • Billing Errors and Service Failures

    If you were charged in error, charged twice, or were unable to use the Services for an extended period due to a fault on our side, contact us and we will issue a refund or credit for the affected amount.

  • Auditor Marketplace and Escrow

    Payments for auditor services arranged through the marketplace are held in escrow. If a job is cancelled before work starts, escrowed funds are returned to the business. Once work has started, refunds of escrowed funds are handled through the in-platform dispute process, taking into account the work delivered.

  • Account Termination

    Refunds are not available if we suspend or terminate an account for a breach of our terms, fraud, or misuse of the Services, except where required by law.

How to Request a Refund

Email support@integribiz.com with your account email address, the date and amount of the payment, and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method and may take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank or card provider.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will post the updated policy on our website or notify you through other communications. Changes do not affect payments made before the update.
